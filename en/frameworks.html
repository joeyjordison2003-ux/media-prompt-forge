Know your model first: DALL-E 3 vs Midjourney

DALL-E 3 / GPT Image

Prefers natural-language description and rewrites short prompts internally. Never stack junk tags like 8k, hyper-realistic or trending on artstation — they trigger waxy CGI faces. Use physical details instead: 35mm film grain, visible skin pores.

Midjourney

Prefers structured phrases plus parameters: --ar for aspect ratio, --style raw to reduce default beautification, --stylize 0-1000, --chaos 0-100. Add background and lighting yourself — it won't invent a story like DALL-E does.

Lens cheat sheet

14–18mm ultra-wide

Dramatic perspective, deep focus, monumental space. Landscapes, brutalist exteriors, sci-fi megacities.

24–35mm wide

Documentary feel that keeps the environment in frame. Street and environmental portraits.

50mm standard

Closest to the human eye, no distortion. Interiors, daily portraits, tabletop food.

85–105mm tele

The classic portrait range — compressed background, creamy bokeh. Close-ups and product beauty shots.

135–200mm long

Heavy compression, razor-thin depth of field. Wildlife and stacked mountain ridges.

The 8 frameworks

PLATE — food & dining

Product plating · Layout & location · Angle & lens · Texture & state · Energy & lighting.

Key words: steam rising, condensation droplets, glistening glaze, matte black ceramic plate.

FACE — portraits

Format & medium · Actor & attributes · Camera & technique · Environment & lighting.

Kill the wax-figure look: visible pores, light freckles, 35mm film grain, soft Rembrandt lighting.

SPACE — architecture & interiors

Structure & style · Perspective · Anchor materials · Color cohesion · Energy & lighting.

Key words: two-point perspective, bare concrete, warm oak, architectural digest style.

PROD — product & e-commerce

Product & packaging · Representative background · Optics & angle · Direction of light.

Key words: rim light silhouette, refracted caustics, basalt stone pedestal, clinging water droplets.

WIND — landscape

Weather & atmosphere · Image composition · Nature's time & seasons · Depth & lenses.

Key words: Tyndall light beams, golden hour, mirror reflection, long exposure, 14mm ultra-wide.

ART — anime & illustration

Aesthetic & school · Render & textures · Theme & palette.

Key words: Ghibli watercolor, Makoto Shinkai, hand-drawn look, visible paper grain, explicit color palette.

VOID — sci-fi concept art

Vehicle & vessel · Out-of-this-world terrain · Illumination & energy · Dramatic scale.

Key words: tiny figure for scale, volumetric lighting, wet asphalt neon reflections, megastructure.

SEEDANCE — video storyboard

8-module timeline formula for AI video: style line, subject, scene, action, camera language, lighting, style reference, stability constraints — every segment must name a shot size and a camera move.
