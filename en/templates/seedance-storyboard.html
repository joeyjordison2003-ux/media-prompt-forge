Standard 15-second five-segment timeline

[Style] ____ style, 15 seconds, 16:9, ____ mood
0–3s: wide establishing shot, slow push-in
3–6s: medium shot, lateral track introduces the subject
6–10s: close tracking shot, core action unfolds slowly and continuously
10–13s: close-up, emotional turn, background slowly defocuses
13–15s: pull-out to full shot, fade transition
[Constraints] consistent face, no teleporting motion, smooth camera, stable light direction

Core principles: write motion slow, not fast (fast action breaks AI video); avoid multi-step actions; describe the subject with concrete physical features instead of empty adjectives.

Shot sizes

Wide

Environment-first vista — opening establishment.

Full

Whole body with full context — subject-environment relationship.

Medium

Knees up — dialogue and action beats.

Close

Chest up — expression and emotion.

Close-up

Face or detail — peak emotion, key props.

POV

First-person — immersion and tension.

Camera moves & transitions

Push / pull

Push-in emphasizes, pull-out reveals.

Pan / track / follow

Sweep the space, travel with the subject.

Orbit / crane

360° hero reveal; vertical scale change.

Hard cut / fade / dissolve

Tension; emotional transition; passage of time.

Slow-mo / time-lapse

Emphasize a gesture; compress time.

Cut to black

Scene change punctuation.

Five stability constraint groups (anti-artifact)

Subject: consistent facial identity, no distortion, natural proportions. Scene: stable background, fixed element positions, no flicker. Action: fluid and continuous, physically plausible joints, no teleporting. Camera: smooth movement, steady framing, subject stays in frame. Lighting: constant light direction, consistent shadows, no exposure flicker.
