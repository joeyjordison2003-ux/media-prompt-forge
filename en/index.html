Prompt Builder

Three-stage audit: junk-tag cleanup (8k, hyper-realistic, masterpiece), aesthetic/motion conflict detection (wabi-sabi × 3D text, serene × motion blur), and a text-budget score that prevents garbled AI typography.

Cinematic Style Library

300 searchable style prompts across film looks, textures, lighting palettes, camera techniques and platform styles — one click inserts them into your prompt.

Model Exporter

Prompt History
